Ministry of Forests, Lands and Natural Resource Operations
BC River Forecast Centre

UPDATE – High Streamflow Advisory: Southwest and Southeast Peace Region
Issued: 13July2011 8:15 AM

The BC River Forecast Centre is downgrading the Flood Watch to a High Streamflow Advisory for:
•      Southeast Peace Region

The BC River Forecast Centre is maintaining the High Streamflow Advisory for:
•     Southwest Peace Region

Rainfall has eased in the Peace Region and river levels have been dropping throughout the region since
Sunday or Monday. Most rivers are now below levels of concern.

Environment Canada is forecasting another low pressure system through the Interior of British Columbia
which may develop additional moisture over the Peace Region. Rainfall amounts in the 15 to 25 mm are
expected, with the potential for higher amounts locally. River levels are expected to have a moderate
rise Thursday and Friday in response to this rainfall.

The River Forecast Centre will continue to monitor conditions and will provide an update as conditions
warrant. Current streamflow information is available from the River Forecast Centre website:
http://bcrfc.env.gov.bc.ca/.




A High Streamflow Advisory means that river levels are rising or expected to rise rapidly, but that no major flooding
is expected. Minor flooding in low-lying areas is possible.

A Flood Watch means that river levels are rising and will approach or may exceed bankfull. Flooding of areas
adjacent to affected rivers may occur.

A Flood Warning means that river levels have exceeded bankfull or will exceed bankfull imminently, and that
flooding of areas adjacent to the rivers affected will result.
