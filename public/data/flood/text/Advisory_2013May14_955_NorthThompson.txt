Ministry of Forests, Lands and Natural Resource Operations
BC River Forecast Centre

High Streamflow Advisory: North Thompson River
Issued: 14 May 2013 10:00 AM

The BC River Forecast Centre is issuing a High Streamflow Advisory for:
     •   North Thompson River
River levels along the North Thompson River have been rising in response to warm
temperatures and heavy snow melt over the past week, and wetter weather over the past
couple of days.
The North Thompson River at Birch Island (08LB047) is currently flowing at 745 m3/s
(approaching a 5‐year flow) and rising. The North Thompson River at McClure (08LB064) is
currently flowing at 1900 m3/s (approaching a 5‐year flow) and rising at 220 m3/s per day.
River levels are expected to peak around Birch Island today, and at McClure tomorrow, with
flows forecast to reach between a 5‐year and 10‐year flow. Beyond Wednesday, river levels are
expected to drop through the rest of the week.
The River Forecast Centre will continue to monitor conditions and will provide an update if
required.




A High Streamflow Advisory means that river levels are rising or expected to rise rapidly, but that no major flooding
is expected. Minor flooding in low-lying areas is possible.

A Flood Watch means that river levels are rising and will approach or may exceed bankfull. Flooding of areas
adjacent to affected rivers may occur.

A Flood Warning means that river levels have exceeded bankfull or will exceed bankfull imminently, and that
flooding of areas adjacent to the rivers affected will result.
