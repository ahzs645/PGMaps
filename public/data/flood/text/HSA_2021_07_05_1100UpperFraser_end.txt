ENDED - Flood Watch – Upper Fraser River
ISSUED: 11:00 AM July 5, 2021

The River Forecast Centre is ending the Flood Watch for the Upper Fraser River including:

    •    Upper Fraser River including the Robson Valley and areas from Sinclair Mills upstream
         to Torpy, Dome Creek, Dore River, McBride and surrounding tributaries

With easing temperatures and decreased snowmelt rates, rivers have reached their peak levels
and are now receding. Flows are now below 2-year return period flows along the mainstem of
the Fraser River through the Robson Valley and surrounding tributaries. Continued recession of
river flow is expected today and into next week.

The River Forecast Centre continues to monitor the conditions and will provide updates as
conditions warrant.


BC River Forecast Centre

A High Streamflow Advisory means that river levels are rising or expected to rise rapidly, but that no major flooding

is expected. Minor flooding in low-lying areas is possible.

A Flood Watch means that river levels are rising and will approach or may exceed bankfull. Flooding of areas

adjacent to affected rivers may occur.

A Flood Warning means that river levels have exceeded bankfull or will exceed bankfull imminently, and that

flooding of areas adjacent to the rivers affected will result.
