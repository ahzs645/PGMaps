Ministry of Forests

High Streamflow Advisory – East Peace
ISSUED: 11:30 AM September 15, 2022

The River Forecast Centre is issuing a High Streamflow Advisory for:

    •    East Peace Region including the Pouce Coupe River, Kiskatinaw River and areas around
         Dawson Creek, Chetwynd and Hudson’s Hope

A low-pressure trough is expected to bring unsettled weather to the Peace Region over the
weekend. Weather modelling is indicating the potential for moderate to heavy rainfall (20-
50+mm) over Saturday and Sunday, with the heavier amounts currently forecasted in areas
south of Dawson Creek. While rainfall is expected to be more moderate in western areas within
the region, including Hudson’s Hope, current wildfire activity, including the Battleship Mountain
and Bearhole Lake fires, may exacerbate localized runoff.

Rivers are expected to respond to rainfall, with rising flows expected on Saturday and reaching
peak levels on Sunday. Current modelling is indicating the potential for localized flows in the 2-
year to 5-year return period range.

Details of forecast from the CLEVER Model can be found at:
http://bcrfc.env.gov.bc.ca/freshet/map_clever.html

The public is advised to stay clear of the fast-flowing rivers and potentially unstable riverbanks
during the high-streamflow period.

The River Forecast Centre continues to monitor the conditions and will provide updates as
conditions warrant.


BC River Forecast Centre
Ministry of Forests
A High Streamflow Advisory means that river levels are rising or expected to rise rapidly, but that no major flooding

is expected. Minor flooding in low-lying areas is possible.

A Flood Watch means that river levels are rising and will approach or may exceed bankfull. Flooding of areas

adjacent to affected rivers may occur.

A Flood Warning means that river levels have exceeded bankfull or will exceed bankfull imminently, and that

flooding of areas adjacent to the rivers affected will result.
