High Streamflow Advisory – Fraser River
ISSUED: 12:45 PM July 2, 2021

The River Forecast Centre is issuing a High Streamflow Advisory for:

   •   Fraser River from Chilcotin River confluence to Mission, including areas through the
       Fraser Canyon, Big Bar, and Hope

Extreme temperatures over the past week has led to rapid snow and glacial melt at high
elevation terrain throughout the mountainous region of the Fraser River watershed. Flows in
key tributaries have seen increased flows over the past several days and is bringing a pulse of
runoff downstream along the Fraser River.

Current conditions include:

   •   FRASER RIVER AT BIG BAR CREEK (WATER SURVEY OF CANADA GAUGE 08MD013) is at an
       estimate 5360 m3/s (between a 2-year and 5-year flow) and is approaching peak levels

   •   FRASER RIVER NEAR HOPE (08MF005) is currently flowing at approximately 8400 m3/s
       (approaching a 2-year return period flow) and is forecast to see additional rises into the
       weekend (flows in the 7900 – 9200 m3/s range through the weekend)

   •   FRASER RIVER AT MISSION (08MH024) is currently at a level of 5.3 m and forecast to rise up to
       5.4-5.5 m over the weekend

CLEVER Model forecasts are available at: http://bcrfc.env.gov.bc.ca/freshet/map_clever.html

Ten-day water level forecasts from the Flood Safety Section in the Ministry of Forests, Lands,
Natural Resource Operations and Rural Development for the Lower Fraser River are available
at: http://bcrfc.env.gov.bc.ca/freshet/lower_fraser/LFR-10-DayFloodLevelForecasts.pdf

The public is advised to stay clear of the fast-flowing rivers and potentially unstable riverbanks
during the high-streamflow period.

The River Forecast Centre continues to monitor the conditions and will provide updates as
conditions warrant.

A High Streamflow Advisory means that river levels are rising or expected to rise rapidly, but that no major flooding

is expected. Minor flooding in low-lying areas is possible.

A Flood Watch means that river levels are rising and will approach or may exceed bankfull. Flooding of areas

adjacent to affected rivers may occur.

A Flood Warning means that river levels have exceeded bankfull or will exceed bankfull imminently, and that

flooding of areas adjacent to the rivers affected will result.
