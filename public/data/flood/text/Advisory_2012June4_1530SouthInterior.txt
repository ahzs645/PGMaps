High Streamflow Advisory: South Interior and Okanagan‐Kettle
Issued: 04June2012 3:45PM

The BC River Forecast Centre is issuing a High Streamflow Advisory for rivers in the Interior
including:
     •   South Interior
     •   Okanagan‐Kettle


A large‐scale upper low pressure system is currently developing across the western United
States and contains significant moisture. This system is expected to deliver significant rain over
Monday evening and through Tuesday for the Okanagan‐Kettle and South Interior region.
Forecasted rainfall amounts over the next 2 to 3 days are in the 30 to 70 mm range, with the
potential for higher amounts locally.
Rivers in the South Interior and Okanagan‐Kettle are expected to begin rising early on Tuesday
and persist rising into Wednesday and potentially Thursday. The timing and amount of rise in
rivers across the region will depend on the amount of rain that falls. At this stage there is still
uncertainty over the overall amounts of rain that will fall, and the locations which will
experience the heaviest rainfall. As a result, river forecasts are expected to change over the
next several days as weather modelling and weather observations continue to track the
progress of this weather event.
The River Forecast Centre will continue to monitor conditions and will provide updates as
conditions warrant.




A High Streamflow Advisory means that river levels are rising or expected to rise rapidly, but that no major flooding
is expected. Minor flooding in low-lying areas is possible.

A Flood Watch means that river levels are rising and will approach or may exceed bankfull. Flooding of areas
adjacent to affected rivers may occur.

A Flood Warning means that river levels have exceeded bankfull or will exceed bankfull imminently, and that
flooding of areas adjacent to the rivers affected will result.
