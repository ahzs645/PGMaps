Flood Watch: North Thompson ‐ Cariboo
UPDATED: 27May2014 12:00PM

The following is the status of flood watches, warnings and advisories in the North Thompson
and Cariboo region as of 27May2014 @ 12:00PM:
     Clearwater River – Flood Warning (UPGRADED)
     Quesnel River – Flood Watch (UPGRADED)
     Horsefly River – Flood Watch (Maintained)
     North Thompson River – High Streamflow Advisory (Maintained)
     Cariboo Mountains and eastern Mid‐Fraser tributaries, including the Cottonwood
      River – High Streamflow Advisory (Maintained)
_____________________________________________________________________________
An upper low system is expected to pass across British Columbia on Wednesday, ushering in a
period of showers through Central and Southern British Columbia. While many areas are
forecast to receive moderate rainfall amounts (5‐15 mm), localized areas may receive upwards
of 40 mm. While many rivers in the region are currently showing signs of easing or dropping,
further rises in levels are expected in response to this forecast rainfall through Thursday.

The Clearwater River near Clearwater Station (08LA001) is currently at 1346 m3/s (20‐year
flow), stage is 5.63 m. Water levels are rising slowly at this time however additional rises are
possible into Thursday, depending on local rainfall amounts.

The Quesnel River near Quesnel (08KH006) is currently flowing at 938 m3/s (between a 5‐year
and 10‐year flow), stage is 4.23 m, and rising at approximately 5 cm/day.

The Horsefly River above McKinley Creek (09KH010) is currently at 134 m3/s (5‐year flow) and
holding steady.

The North Thompson at McLure (Water Survey of Canada gauge 08LB064) is currently flowing
at 2167 m3/s (between a 5‐year and 10‐year flow). River levels are now dropping. Additional
rises are possible on Wednesday through Friday, depending on rainfall amounts in tributary
watersheds.



A High Streamflow Advisory means that river levels are rising or expected to rise rapidly, but that no major flooding
is expected. Minor flooding in low-lying areas is possible.

A Flood Watch means that river levels are rising and will approach or may exceed bankfull. Flooding of areas
adjacent to affected rivers may occur.

A Flood Warning means that river levels have exceeded bankfull or will exceed bankfull imminently, and that
flooding of areas adjacent to the rivers affected will result.
