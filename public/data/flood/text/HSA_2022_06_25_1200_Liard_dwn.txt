Ministry of Forests

High Streamflow Advisory - Northeast BC, Liard River (DOWNGRADED)
High Streamflow Advisory – Swift River and Northwest BC (MAINTAINED)
ISSUED: 12:00 PM June 25, 2022

The River Forecast Centre is downgrading to a High Streamflow Advisory for:

    •    Northeast British Columbia including areas around Fort Nelson and the Northern
         Rocky Mountains

    •    Liard River including tributaries around Fort Nelson and Highway 97 towards Watson
         Lake

The River Forecast Centre is maintaining to a High Streamflow Advisory for:

    •    Swift River, and other streams and rivers in the most northwest section of the
         province

Flows have generally been easing through the region over the past few days. Rivers are
expected to continue to remain stable. In the Northwest, small rises in river levels are expected
over the weekend. In the Liard River and the Northeast, rises in flows are possible early next
week in response to forecasted rainfall.

The public is advised to stay clear of the fast-flowing rivers and potentially unstable riverbanks
during the high-streamflow period. Be prepared and know your hazards.

The River Forecast Centre continues to monitor the conditions and will provide updates as
conditions warrant.


BC River Forecast Centre
Ministry of Forests


A High Streamflow Advisory means that river levels are rising or expected to rise rapidly, but that no major flooding

is expected. Minor flooding in low-lying areas is possible.

A Flood Watch means that river levels are rising and will approach or may exceed bankfull. Flooding of areas

adjacent to affected rivers may occur.

A Flood Warning means that river levels have exceeded bankfull or will exceed bankfull imminently, and that

flooding of areas adjacent to the rivers affected will result.
