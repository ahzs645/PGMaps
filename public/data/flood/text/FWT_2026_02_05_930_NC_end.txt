Ministry of Water, Land and Resource Stewardship

ENDED - Flood Watch – North Coast
ENDED - High Streamflow Advisory – Central Coast
Issued: 9:30 AM February 5, 2026


The River Forecast Centre is ending to a Flood Watch for:

    •    North Coast including tributaries along the outer coast and inland areas including Prince
         Rupert, Kitimat, Terrace and surrounding areas

The River Forecast Centre is ending a High Streamflow Advisory for:

    •    Central Coast including tributaries north of Klemtu

Weather Synopsis:

Rainfall amounts of 50 to 155 mm since February 3 were observed in the region. Rainfall rates
have now eased. Snowmelt rates of 20 to 40 mm were also observed over this period.

River Conditions:

Rivers have reached peak levels, with maximum flows in the 2-year to above a 5-year flow
observed at river gauges in the region. Flows are now receding and are below levels of concern.
No significant additional rises are anticipated at this time.

The River Forecast Centre continues to monitor the conditions and will provide updates as
conditions warrant.

For information on how to prepare for flood hazards, visit PreparedBC.


BC River Forecast Centre
Ministry of Water, Land and Resource Stewardship
A High Streamflow Advisory means that river levels are rising or expected to rise rapidly, but that no major flooding

is expected. Fast-flowing bodies of water increase risk to life safety. Minor flooding in low-lying areas is possible.
A Flood Watch means that river levels are rising and will approach or may exceed bankfull. Flooding of areas

adjacent to affected rivers may occur.

A Flood Warning means that river levels have exceeded bankfull or will exceed bankfull imminently, and that

flooding of areas adjacent to the rivers affected will result.
