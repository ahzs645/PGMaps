Ministry of Forests, Lands and Natural Resource Operations
BC River Forecast Centre

ENDED ‐ Flood Watch: Birkenhead River
ENDED ‐ High Streamflow Advisory: South Coast
Updated: 14 May 2013 9:45 AM

The BC River Forecast Centre is ending the Flood Watch and High Streamflow Advisory for:
     •   Birkenhead River
     •   Lillooet River and tributaries
     •   Squamish River
     •   Tributary creeks through the Sea‐to‐Sky Corridor and Pemberton Valley
Temperatures have cooled significantly since yesterday, leading to diminished snow melt rates.
Rainfall rates observed at automated snow pillows (Upper Squamish River, Tenquille Lake) over
the past 24‐hours have been in the 10‐20 mm range.
Some rivers saw a modest rise yesterday and overnight, but river levels throughout the region
are now dropping. Snow melt rates are expected to remain low through the rest of the week,
with modest rainfall in the forecast. River levels are expected to continue to decline.
The River Forecast Centre will continue to monitor conditions and provide updates if required.




A High Streamflow Advisory means that river levels are rising or expected to rise rapidly, but that no major flooding
is expected. Minor flooding in low-lying areas is possible.

A Flood Watch means that river levels are rising and will approach or may exceed bankfull. Flooding of areas
adjacent to affected rivers may occur.

A Flood Warning means that river levels have exceeded bankfull or will exceed bankfull imminently, and that
flooding of areas adjacent to the rivers affected will result.
