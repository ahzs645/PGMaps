Ministry of Forests

UPDATE: High Streamflow Advisory – Fraser Valley (DOWNGRADED)
ENDED - Flood Watch – Howe Sound, North Shore, Metro Vancouver
ENDED - High Streamflow Advisory – South Coast
Issued: 12:30 PM December 26, 2022

The River Forecast Centre is downgrading to a High Streamflow Advisory for:

    •    Fraser Valley including Sumas River

The River Forecast Centre is ending to Flood Watches High Streamflow Advisories for:

    •    Howe Sound

    •    North Shore Mountains

    •    Metro Vancouver

    •    South Coast including the Sunshine Coast and surrounding tributaries

Rainfall has eased across the region. River levels in most areas have peaked and are now
receding.

In the Fraser Valley, low-lying rivers, including the Sumas River, remain elevated due to a slower
drainage. River levels may see small additional rises today, with conditions expected to improve
into Thursday.

The River Forecast Centre continues to monitor the conditions and will provide updates as
conditions warrant.


BC River Forecast Centre
Ministry of Forests
A High Streamflow Advisory means that river levels are rising or expected to rise rapidly, but that no major flooding

is expected. Minor flooding in low-lying areas is possible.

A Flood Watch means that river levels are rising and will approach or may exceed bankfull. Flooding of areas

adjacent to affected rivers may occur.

A Flood Warning means that river levels have exceeded bankfull or will exceed bankfull imminently, and that

flooding of areas adjacent to the rivers affected will result.
