High Streamflow Advisory – Peace Region
Maintained: 15:30 PM June 4, 2021

The River Forecast Centre is maintaining the High Streamflow Advisory for the Peace region,
including:

    •    Rivers draining into the Williston Lake

    •    The Pine River and tributaries

Continuous high temperatures up to 26°C early this week have triggered significant amounts of
snowmelt in the Peace Region, which have caused significant rises in the river levels. Many
watersheds with large areas at higher elevations are currently running at flows between their 2-
5-year return periods. According to the current hydrologic modeling, larger, downstream sites
are expected to peak into this weekend between their 2-5 year return period flows; smaller
systems area likely to peak today. Streams that are high due to snowmelt are vulnerable to
significant rainfall until they recede to lower levels. Details of the CLEVER Model forecasts for
this region can be found at: http://bcrfc.env.gov.bc.ca/freshet/map_clever.html

The public is advised to stay clear of the fast-flowing rivers and potentially unstable riverbanks
during the high-streamflow period.

The River Forecast Centre continues to monitor the conditions and will provide updates as
conditions warrant.

A High Streamflow Advisory means that river levels are rising or expected to rise rapidly, but that no major flooding

is expected. Minor flooding in low-lying areas is possible.

A Flood Watch means that river levels are rising and will approach or may exceed bankfull. Flooding of areas

adjacent to affected rivers may occur.

A Flood Warning means that river levels have exceeded bankfull or will exceed bankfull imminently, and that

flooding of areas adjacent to the rivers affected will result.
