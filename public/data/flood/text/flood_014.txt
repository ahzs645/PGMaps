After a data correction (estimation) by the Water Survey of Canada (WSC) , the CHILCOTIN RIVER BELOW BIG CREEK (08MB005) is currently recording a flow of 1090 m3/s, which is a flow over the 200-year return period. According to Environment and Climate Change Canada (ECCC), additional rainfall about 5 mm is expected for the Chilcotin region today. The CLEVER Model updated this morning indicates that the river level is expected to rise slightly and peak today and then recede slowly. The public is advised to stay clear of the torrential rivers and potentially unstable riverbanks. The River Forecast Centre will continue to monitor the conditions and update this advisory as conditions warrant. Contact for Media Relations, Ministry of Forests, Lands, Natural Resource Operations and Rural Development: Tel: 250 356-7506 Levels of Warnings/Advisories: High Streamflow Advisory: River levels are rising or expected to rise rapidly, but that no major flooding is expected. Minor flooding in low-lying areas is possible. This is the lowest level. Flood Watch: River levels are rising and will approach or may exceed bankfull. Flooding of areas adjacent to affected rivers may occur. This is the intermediate level. Flood Warning: River levels have exceeded bankfull or will exceed bankfull imminently, and that flooding of areas adjacent to the rivers affected will result. This is the highest level.
