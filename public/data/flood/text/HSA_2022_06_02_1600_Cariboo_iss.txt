Ministry of Forests

High Streamflow Advisory – Cariboo
ISSUED: 4:00 PM June 2, 2022


The River Forecast Centre is issuing a High Streamflow Advisory for:

    •    Cariboo Plateau including the Nazko River, West Road River, Baker Creek and
         tributaries to the west of Quesnel

    •    Thompson Region including the Deadman River, Bonaparte River, Cache Creek and
         surrounding tributaries

    •    Dean River

Steady warming this week has led to increased snowmelt and runoff in rivers in the region. A
low-pressure system is expected to impact BC on Friday and into the weekend, bringing
moderate to heavy rainfall throughout the region.

River levels are expected to experience rapid rises on Friday and into Saturday in response to
the rainfall. Some modelling is indicating the potential for extremely high flows (exceeding a 2-
year to 5-year level) however there remain a high degree of uncertainty in the location and
amounts of rainfall.

The public is advised to stay clear of the fast-flowing rivers and potentially unstable riverbanks
during the high-streamflow period. Be prepared and know your hazards.

The River Forecast Centre continues to monitor the conditions and will provide updates as
conditions warrant.


BC River Forecast Centre
Ministry of Forests

A High Streamflow Advisory means that river levels are rising or expected to rise rapidly, but that no major flooding

is expected. Minor flooding in low-lying areas is possible.

A Flood Watch means that river levels are rising and will approach or may exceed bankfull. Flooding of areas

adjacent to affected rivers may occur.

A Flood Warning means that river levels have exceeded bankfull or will exceed bankfull imminently, and that

flooding of areas adjacent to the rivers affected will result.
