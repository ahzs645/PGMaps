Ministry of Forests

ENDED - High Streamflow Advisory – Fraser River
ISSUED: 11:30 AM July 19, 2022

The River Forecast Centre is ending a High Streamflow Advisory for:

    •    Fraser River from Prince George downstream, including Quesnel, Big Bar, Boston Bar,
         and the Fraser Valley from Hope to the ocean

The Fraser River at Hope (08MF005) reached above a 2-year return period flow (8,650 m3/s) on
June 8th and remained above that level until Sunday, July 17th. Flows along the Fraser River
mainstem are receding and are expected to continue through the summer. The upcoming
weather forecast is predicting warm and dry conditions for the next 7-to-10 days. Although the
Fraser River is receding, the flows are very high for late July. Currently, the river is flowing at
approximately the 96th percentile for this time of year. Expect flows to remain higher than
normal through the summer and early fall.

The public is advised to stay clear of the fast-flowing rivers and potentially unstable riverbanks
during the high-streamflow period. Be prepared and know your hazards.

The River Forecast Centre continues to monitor the conditions and will provide updates as
conditions warrant.


BC River Forecast Centre
Ministry of Forests

A High Streamflow Advisory means that river levels are rising or expected to rise rapidly, but that no major flooding

is expected. Minor flooding in low-lying areas is possible.

A Flood Watch means that river levels are rising and will approach or may exceed bankfull. Flooding of areas

adjacent to affected rivers may occur.

A Flood Warning means that river levels have exceeded bankfull or will exceed bankfull imminently, and that

flooding of areas adjacent to the rivers affected will result.
