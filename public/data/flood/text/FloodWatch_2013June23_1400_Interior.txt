Ministry of Forests, Lands and Natural Resource Operations
BC River Forecast Centre

UPDATE:
Flood Watch: Elk River (Downgraded)
High Streamflow Advisory: Interior (Ended)
Issued: 23 June 2013 02:00 PM

The BC River Forecast Centre is downgrading the Flood Warning to a Flood Watch for:
     •   Elk River (at Fernie)
The BC River Forecast Centre is ending the High Streamflow Advisory for:
     •   Upper Columbia (Kicking Horse River)
     •   West and East Kootenay
     •   Upper Columbia (small tributary rivers)
     •   Lower Columbia (small tributary rivers)
The Elk River has been responding to heavy rainfall over the last week and the water level at
Fernie (08NK002) peaked on Friday, June 21, 2013 at 4.625m and flow of approximately 1000
m3/s. The river is now flowing at 506 m3/s and is continuing to recede however river levels
remain very high.
Flows of the Kicking Horse River, the Kootenay River, the lower and upper Columbia Rivers and
their tributaries are now receding and are lower than a flow of 2‐year return period.
The River Forecast Centre will continue to monitor conditions and will provide updates as
required.




A High Streamflow Advisory means that river levels are rising or expected to rise rapidly, but that no major flooding
is expected. Minor flooding in low-lying areas is possible.

A Flood Watch means that river levels are rising and will approach or may exceed bankfull. Flooding of areas
adjacent to affected rivers may occur.

A Flood Warning means that river levels have exceeded bankfull or will exceed bankfull imminently, and that
flooding of areas adjacent to the rivers affected will result.
