Ended: High Streamflow Advisory – Peace Region and Liard River
Ended: 11:30 AM June 7, 2021

The River Forecast Centre is Ending the High Streamflow Advisory for the Peace region and the
Liard River, including:

    •    Rivers draining into the Williston Lake

    •    The Pine River and tributaries

    •    COTTONWOOD RIVER ABOVE BASS CREEK (10AC005)

    •    Liard River and tributaries above the Hyland River

Rivers in the above regions have peaked at flows about the 5-year return period on the past
weekend. Based on the current hydrological modeling, the river levels are expected to drop
further this week, though minor rises are possible. Details of the CLEVER Model forecasts for
this region can be found at: http://bcrfc.env.gov.bc.ca/freshet/map_clever.html

The River Forecast Centre continues to monitor the conditions and will provide updates as
conditions warrant.

A High Streamflow Advisory means that river levels are rising or expected to rise rapidly, but that no major flooding

is expected. Minor flooding in low-lying areas is possible.

A Flood Watch means that river levels are rising and will approach or may exceed bankfull. Flooding of areas

adjacent to affected rivers may occur.

A Flood Warning means that river levels have exceeded bankfull or will exceed bankfull imminently, and that

flooding of areas adjacent to the rivers affected will result.
