Ministry of Forests, Lands and Natural Resource Operations
BC River Forecast Centre

Flood Warning: Upper Fraser River
REVISED: 14 May 2013 10:40 AM

The BC River Forecast Centre is upgrading the advisory to a Flood Warning for:
     •   Upper Fraser River (at Prince George and upstream)
River levels along the Upper Fraser have risen since yesterday. Temperatures cooled yesterday,
however snowmelt rates remained moderate.
Current river levels (as of 8AM) are as follows:
     •   McGregor River at Lower Canyon (WSC Gauge 08KB003) is flowing at 1660 m3/s and
         gauge height 4.76 m (approximately a 10‐year flow) and peaked at around midnight last
         night. The river is now slowly dropping and is expected to continue dropping through
         today.
     •   Fraser River at Hansard (08KA004) is flowing at 2240 m3/s and gauge height of 7.21m
         (between a 2‐year and 5‐year flow). The Fraser River at Hansard is expected to peak this
         afternoon at 2300‐2400 m3/s, or a gauge height of approximately 7.4‐7.6m.
     •   Fraser River at Shelley (08KB001) is flowing at 4250 m3/s and gauge height of 7.66m
         (10‐year flow). The Fraser River at Shelley is expected to rise through this afternoon,
         and then have a long peak through this evening at approximately 4500‐4600 m3/s, or a
         gauge height of approximately 7.8m.
     •   Fraser River at South Fort George (08KE018) is currently at 9.22 m (as of 3AM) and
         rising at 2.5 cm/hr. The Fraser River at South Fort George is expected to rise through
         this afternoon, reaching 9.4 ‐ 9.5m by this afternoon. Additional modest increases are
         expected into the evening, and a revised peak forecast will be issued later today.
Once rivers reach peak levels today and overnight, conditions are expected to stabilize with
river levels dropping tomorrow and through the rest of the week.
The River Forecast Centre will continue to monitor conditions and will provide an update by
5:00 PM today.




A High Streamflow Advisory means that river levels are rising or expected to rise rapidly, but that no major flooding
is expected. Minor flooding in low-lying areas is possible.

A Flood Watch means that river levels are rising and will approach or may exceed bankfull. Flooding of areas
adjacent to affected rivers may occur.

A Flood Warning means that river levels have exceeded bankfull or will exceed bankfull imminently, and that
flooding of areas adjacent to the rivers affected will result.
