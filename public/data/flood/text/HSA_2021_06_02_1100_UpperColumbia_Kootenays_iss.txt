High Streamflow Advisory – Upper Columbia, East Kootenay, West Kootenay
Issued: 11:00AM June 2, 2021

The River Forecast Centre is issuing a High Streamflow Advisory for:

        Upper Columbia

        East Kootenay

        West Kootenay

A strong high‐pressure ridge has developed over British Columbia resulting in the hottest
temperatures of the season. The Upper Columbia, East Kootenay and West Kootenay
accumulated average snowpack during the winter season. The recent heat is rapidly melting
remaining snow at higher elevations and many watersheds are forecast to reach 2 to 5‐year
return period flows. Currently, there is no imminent threat of major flooding. However, the hot
weather is producing the highest flows of the season for the regions and could make the region
vulnerable if a heavy rainfall event were to occur after the heat.

Please refer to the CLEVER forecast page (http://bcrfc.env.gov.bc.ca/freshet/map_clever.html)
for details.

The public is advised to stay clear of the fast‐flowing rivers and potentially unstable riverbanks
during the high‐streamflow period.

The River Forecast Centre continues to monitor the conditions and will provide updates as
conditions warrant.


BC River Forecast Centre
Ministry of Forests, Lands, Natural Resource Operations and Rural Development
A High Streamflow Advisory means that river levels are rising or expected to rise rapidly, but that no major flooding

is expected. Minor flooding in low-lying areas is possible.

A Flood Watch means that river levels are rising and will approach or may exceed bankfull. Flooding of areas

adjacent to affected rivers may occur.

A Flood Warning means that river levels have exceeded bankfull or will exceed bankfull imminently, and that

flooding of areas adjacent to the rivers affected will result.
