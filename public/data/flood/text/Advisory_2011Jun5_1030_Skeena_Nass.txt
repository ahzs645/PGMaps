Ministry of Forests, Lands and Natural Resource Operations
BC River Forecast Centre

ENDED - High Streamflow Advisory : Skeena and Nass Rivers

Issued: 05Jun2011 10:30AM

The BC River Forecast Centre is ending the High Streamflow Advisory for:
        Skeena River
        Nass River
River levels on the Skeena River have been dropping, and are now below levels of concern. The
most recent river level at the Skeena River at Usk (WSC 08EF001) at 0800h is 9.01 m with a
discharge of 4474 m3/s, a drop of about 1 m from its peak on June 3 rd.
On the Nass River, water levels have also been dropping since their peak on June 3rd. The Nass
River above Schumal Creek (WSC 08DB001) is currently at 4.71 m, with a discharge of 2587
m3/s.
Environment Canada is forecasting a frontal system to bring cooler temperatures and light
precipitation on Monday night into Tuesday with freezing levels dropping to 1500 m. Following
this system is drier weather and slightly above seasonal temperatures with freezing levels rising
back up to 2000 m towards the end of the week. River levels are expected to continue to fall
over the next few days.
The River Forecast Centre will continue to monitor stream flow conditions and provide updates
as necessary.




A High Streamflow Advisory means that river levels are rising or expected to rise rapidly, but that no major flooding
is expected. Minor flooding in low-lying areas is possible.

A Flood Watch means that river levels are rising and will approach or may exceed bankfull. Flooding of areas
adjacent to affected rivers may occur.

A Flood Warning means that river levels have exceeded bankfull or will exceed bankfull imminently, and that
flooding of areas adjacent to the rivers affected will result.
