Ministry of Forests, Lands and Natural Resource Operations
BC River Forecast Centre

High Streamflow Advisory: Howe Sound (South) and North Shore Mountains
Issued: 26September2011 6:45PM

The BC River Forecast Centre is issuing a High Streamflow Advisory for:
     •   Howe Sound – South
     •   North Shore Mountains (including Seymour River, Capilano Creek, Lynn Creek and
         Coquitlam River and surrounding areas)
Heavy rain has been falling through the day in Howe Sound and areas of the North Shore
Mountains. Rainfall rates over the past 12 hours has been in 38 mm at the MoTI Weather
Station at Mt. Strachan and 66 mm at the Water Survey of Canada gauge at Coquitlam River
above Coquitlam Lake (08MH141).
Rivers have been responding to this rainfall. The Coquitlam River above Coquitlam Lake
(08MH141) is currently at 72 m3/s, and is rising at 15 cm/hour. The Seymour River below Orchid
Creek is currently at 79 m3/s, and rising at 10 cm/hour.
Environment Canada has issued a rainfall warning for Howe Sound, with additional rainfall
amounts of 40 mm to 60 mm forecasted through Tuesday morning. Rivers are expected to
continue to rise overnight in response to this rain.
The River Forecast Centre will continue to monitor conditions and will provide updates as
conditions warrant.




A High Streamflow Advisory means that river levels are rising or expected to rise rapidly, but that no major flooding
is expected. Minor flooding in low-lying areas is possible.

A Flood Watch means that river levels are rising and will approach or may exceed bankfull. Flooding of areas
adjacent to affected rivers may occur.

A Flood Warning means that river levels have exceeded bankfull or will exceed bankfull imminently, and that
flooding of areas adjacent to the rivers affected will result.
