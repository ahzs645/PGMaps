Upgraded: Flood Watch – Chilcotin
ISSUED: 07:30 AM July 29, 2022

The River Forecast Centre is upgrading the High Streamflow Advisory to a Flood Watch for:

    •    Chilcotin Region including the Chilcotin River, Chilko River and tributaries

Hot temperatures in early this week have triggered significant of snowpack and glacier melt at
the high elevations of the Chilcotin Basin. The Water Survey of Canada (WSC) real-time
hydrometric station CHILCOTIN RIVER NEAR HANCEVILLE (08MB012) is currently running at
about 400 m3/s, a flow slightly over the 10-year return period level and expected to rise further
today into the weekend. The other WSC stations in the Chilcotin River and tributaries upstream
of Hanceville are also running high at flows from 2- to 10-year return periods and expected to
continue to rise today into the weekend. Details of the CLEVER Model forecast can be found at:
http://bcrfc.env.gov.bc.ca/freshet/map_clever.html

The public is advised to stay clear of the fast-flowing rivers and potentially unstable riverbanks
during the high-streamflow period. Be prepared and know your hazards.

The River Forecast Centre continues to monitor the conditions and will provide updates as
conditions warrant.


BC River Forecast Centre
Ministry of Forests

A High Streamflow Advisory means that river levels are rising or expected to rise rapidly, but that no major flooding

is expected. Minor flooding in low-lying areas is possible.

A Flood Watch means that river levels are rising and will approach or may exceed bankfull. Flooding of areas

adjacent to affected rivers may occur.

A Flood Warning means that river levels have exceeded bankfull or will exceed bankfull imminently, and that

flooding of areas adjacent to the rivers affected will result.
