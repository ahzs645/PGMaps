Thunderstorms with rainfall less than 5 mm is expected for the Chilcotin region today. The CLEVER Model updated this morning indicates that the river level is expected to recede today and for the rest of the week. The CHILKO RIVER NEAR REDSTONE (08MA001) is currently running at a flow of 380 m3/s, which is flow between the 5 and 10-year return periods and the water level is dropping slowly. The BIG CREEK ABOVE GROUNDHOG CREEK (08MB006) is currently running at a flow of 48 m3/s, which is a flow slightly over the 2-year return period, and the water level is dropping. The public is advised to stay clear of the torrential rivers and potentially unstable riverbanks. The River Forecast Centre will continue to monitor the conditions and update this advisory as conditions warrant. Contact for Media Relations, Ministry of Forests, Lands, Natural Resource Operations and Rural Development: Tel: 250 356-7506 Levels of Warnings/Advisories: High Streamflow Advisory: River levels are rising or expected to rise rapidly, but that no major flooding is expected. Minor flooding in low-lying areas is possible. This is the lowest level. Flood Watch: River levels are rising and will approach or may exceed bankfull. Flooding of areas adjacent to affected rivers may occur. This is the intermediate level. Flood Warning: River levels have exceeded bankfull or will exceed bankfull imminently, and that flooding of areas adjacent to the rivers affected will result. This is the highest level.
