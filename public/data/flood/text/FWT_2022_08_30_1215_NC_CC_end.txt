Ministry of Forests

ENDED - Flood Watch – North Coast
ENDED - High Streamflow Advisory – Central Coast
Issued: 12:15PM August 30, 2022

The River Forecast Centre is ending a Flood Watch for:

    •    North Coast

The River Forecast Centre is ending a High Streamflow Advisory for:

    •    Central Coast

A moderate to strong intensity atmospheric river impacted the Central and North Coast of
British Columbia over the past few days. Although rainfall amounts were relatively high, rivers
did not rise as quickly as yesterday’s hydrological forecast. The preceding dry conditions likely
limited flashy rivers responses from the heavy rain. Also, limited real-time hydrometric
monitoring is available in the most impacted areas. Drier conditions are expected later in the
week and rivers are forecast to recede after the recent rises.

The public is advised to stay clear of the fast-flowing rivers and potentially unstable riverbanks
during the high-streamflow period.

The River Forecast Centre continues to monitor the conditions and will provide updates as
conditions warrant.


BC River Forecast Centre
Ministry of Forests
A High Streamflow Advisory means that river levels are rising or expected to rise rapidly, but that no major flooding

is expected. Minor flooding in low-lying areas is possible.

A Flood Watch means that river levels are rising and will approach or may exceed bankfull. Flooding of areas

adjacent to affected rivers may occur.

A Flood Warning means that river levels have exceeded bankfull or will exceed bankfull imminently, and that

flooding of areas adjacent to the rivers affected will result.
