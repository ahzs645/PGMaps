Ministry of Forests, Lands and Natural Resource Operations
BC River Forecast Centre (http://bcrfc.env.gov.bc.ca/)

High Streamflow Advisory: Central Coast
ISSUED: 06October2013 11:30AM

The BC River Forecast Centre is issuing a High Streamflow Advisory for the Central Coast
including:
     •   Kingcome River
Central and northern coastal British Columbia is currently being impacted by a number of
Pacific storm systems. Heavy rainfall over the past 48 hours has led to elevated river levels
throughout the region. Rain has eased today through the central coast, and this is allowing for
river levels to ease slightly.
The Kingcome River below Atlazi River (WSC 08GF009) has dropped from a high overnight level
of 7.4m, down to a current level of 6.3m.
Another system of heavy rainfall is expected later on Sunday and overnight into Monday.
Rainfall amounts in the 40‐60mm range are possible through the central coast. Additional rises
are expected on the Kingcome River overnight in response to this additional rainfall. River levels
are expected to peak overnight or early Monday, with forecasted levels at the Water Survey of
Canada gauge (08GF009) reaching the 7.0‐8.0m range. The amount of rise will depend on
rainfall rates later today and overnight, and higher levels are possible.
The River Forecast Centre will continue to monitor conditions and will provide updates as
conditions warrant.




A High Streamflow Advisory means that river levels are rising or expected to rise rapidly, but that no major flooding
is expected. Minor flooding in low-lying areas is possible.

A Flood Watch means that river levels are rising and will approach or may exceed bankfull. Flooding of areas
adjacent to affected rivers may occur.

A Flood Warning means that river levels have exceeded bankfull or will exceed bankfull imminently, and that
flooding of areas adjacent to the rivers affected will result.
