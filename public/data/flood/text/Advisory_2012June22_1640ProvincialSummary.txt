A High Streamflow Advisory means that river levels are rising or expected to rise rapidly, but that no major flooding is
expected. Minor flooding in low-lying areas is possible.

A Flood Watch means that river levels are rising and will approach or may exceed bankfull. Flooding of areas adjacent
to affected rivers may occur.

A Flood Warning means that river levels have exceeded bankfull or will exceed bankfull imminently, and that flooding of
areas adjacent to the rivers affected will result.

Rivers have been responding to rapid snowmelt and wet conditions over the past 2‐3 weeks.
Warmer weather over the past 2 days has led to a rapid rise in snowmelt rates through most of
the province where snowpacks remain. A low pressure system off Washington is now bringing
warm and wet weather across British Columbia. The heaviest rainfall, including localized
thunderstorms, is currently expected in the Kootenay, Okanagan, Monashee, Cariboo, and
Chilcotin, with moderate rainfall forecast elsewhere. A summary of current conditions:
    •    Fraser River at South Fort George (08KE018) has dropped from a peak of 10.02 m on
         Wednesday to 9.1 m this morning. The river is expected to stop dropping today. Additional
         rises are expected later on in the weekend and into next week in response to rapid
         snowmelt over the past 2 days, and the rainfall forecast for the weekend. The amount of
         additional rise will depend on the amount of rain over the weekend. The river may return
         to flood stage. Current modelling suggests that the river may rise to the 9.4‐10.0 m level by
         early next week.
    •    The Bulkley River at Smithers (08EE005) is currently at a level of 4.85 m with flows of 800
         m3/s, and has risen approximately 20 cm since Thursday. Additional rises of 10‐40 cm are
         expected through Monday.
    •    The Skeena River at Usk (08EF001) is currently at 5100 m3/s (2‐year flow) and has risen
         nearly 1000 m3/s (or 1 metre) since Thursday. Additional rises of 1000 m3/s (1.0 m) or more
         are expected through Monday or Tuesday.
    •    Shuswap Lake at Salmon Arm (08LE070) is at 349.12 m (15‐year level) and rising at 3
         cm/day. The rate of rise increased slightly from yesterday due to rapid snowmelt over the
         past 2 days. With current rain forecasts, additional rises of 10‐25 cm are expected through
         the early part of next week.
    •    South Thompson River at Chase (08LE031) is at 1315 m3/s (15‐year flow). The river is
         expected to see additional rises due to rainfall over the weekend. The amount and rate of
         rise and ultimate peak level will depend on the how much rain occurs.
    •    The Bella Coola River at Hammer Creek (08FB011) is currently at 4.20 m, and expected to
         see rises up to 1.5 m through Sunday‐Monday
