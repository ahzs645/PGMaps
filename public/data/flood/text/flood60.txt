Home » FLNRO » River Forecast Centre Ministry of FLNRO Flood Warnings and Advisories FLOOD WATCH – Bulkley River (MAINTAINED) UPDATE: May 13, 2017 8:15 AM The River Forecast Centre is maintaining a Flood Watch for: Bulkley River including small streams and tributaries around Houston, Telkwa, Smithers and surrounding areas A prolonged rain event has passed through the region over the past two days. Rainfall totals observed at fire weather stations and automated snow weather stations has been in the 25-60mm range. Rivers in the region have been responding to this rainfall, with rapid rises observed since Friday. Current conditions include: Bulkley River at Quick (08EE004) is flowing at 590 m3/s (between a 2-year and 5-year flow). Flows are still rising, and currently forecasted to remain below 800 m3/s, reaching peak levels this afternoon. Bulkley River near Smithers (08EE005 ) is flowing at 800 m3/s (5-year flow). Flows are still rising, and currently forecasted to remain below 1000 m3/s, reaching peak levels this afternoon or evening. The River Forecast Centre will continue to monitor conditions and will update the advisory as conditions warrant. A High Streamflow Advisory means that river levels are rising or expected to rise rapidly, but that no major flooding is expected. Minor flooding in low-lying areas is possible. A Flood Watch means that river levels are rising and will approach or may exceed bankfull. Flooding of areas adjacent to affected rivers may occur. A Flood Warning means that river levels have exceeded bankfull or will exceed bankfull imminently, and that flooding of areas adjacent to the rivers affected will result.
