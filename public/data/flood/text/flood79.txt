Home » FLNRO » River Forecast Centre Ministry of FLNRO Flood Warnings and Advisories ENDED - High Streamflow Advisory – Nautley River ISSUED: June 3, 2017 10:00 AM The River Forecast Centre is ending a High Streamflow Advisory for: Nautley River including upstream tributaries including Francois Lake and Fraser Lake River levels have peaked and are now receding. Flows are expected to continue to decline through the weekend, and are now below levels of concern. The River Forecast Centre will continue to monitor conditions and will update the advisory as conditions warrant. A High Streamflow Advisory means that river levels are rising or expected to rise rapidly, but that no major flooding is expected. Minor flooding in low-lying areas is possible. A Flood Watch means that river levels are rising and will approach or may exceed bankfull. Flooding of areas adjacent to affected rivers may occur. A Flood Warning means that river levels have exceeded bankfull or will exceed bankfull imminently, and that flooding of areas adjacent to the rivers affected will result.
