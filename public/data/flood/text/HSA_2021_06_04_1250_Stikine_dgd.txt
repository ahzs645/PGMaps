Downgraded – High Streamflow Advisory: Stikine River, Tuya River
Issued: 12:50PM June 4, 2021

The River Forecast Centre is downgrading the Flood Warning to a High Streamflow Advisory
for:

       •   Stikine River and tributaries

       •   Tuya River

Responding to the high temperatures in this region early this week, the Tuya River near
Telegraph Creek (08CD001) has peaked at 690 m3/s, a flow slightly below the 50-year return
period, yesterday, and dropped to a flow between the 5- and 10-year return periods now. The
Stikine River at Telegraph Creek (08CE001) has peaked at 2,590 m3/s, a flow between the 2-year
and 5-year return periods, early this morning. Based on the current hydrological modeling, the
Tuya River is expected to drop further into the weekend, and the Stikine is also expected to
drop slowly this weekend. Minor rises in the river levels could be possible. Please refer to the
CLEVER forecast page (http://bcrfc.env.gov.bc.ca/freshet/map_clever.html) for details.

The public is advised to stay clear of the fast-flowing rivers and potentially unstable riverbanks
during the high-streamflow period.

The River Forecast Centre continues to monitor the conditions and will provide updates as
conditions warrant.


BC River Forecast Centre
Ministry of Forests, Lands, Natural Resource Operations and Rural Development
A High Streamflow Advisory means that river levels are rising or expected to rise rapidly, but that no major flooding

is expected. Minor flooding in low-lying areas is possible.

A Flood Watch means that river levels are rising and will approach or may exceed bankfull. Flooding of areas

adjacent to affected rivers may occur.

A Flood Warning means that river levels have exceeded bankfull or will exceed bankfull imminently, and that

flooding of areas adjacent to the rivers affected will result.
