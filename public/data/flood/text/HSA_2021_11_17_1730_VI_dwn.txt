MAINTAINED – High Streamflow Advisory - Cowichan River
ENDED - High Streamflow Advisory – Englishman River, Koksilah River,
Chemainus River
UPDATE: 17:30 PM November 17, 2021


The River Forecast Centre is maintaining a High Streamflow Advisory for:

    •    Cowichan River

The River Forecast Centre is ending High Streamflow Advisories for:

    •    Englishman River

    •    Koksilah River

    •    Chemainus River

Rivers across Vancouver Island have continued to recede following last weekend’s atmospheric
river event. Flows within the Englishman, Koksilah and Chemainus rivers have all returned to
typical flow levels for this time of year. The Cowichan River is flowing at a slightly greater flow
threshold, owing to the large upstream reservoir system that delays flow recession. Favourable
weather indicates continued recession within streams throughout the region within the short
term.

The public is advised to stay clear of the fast-flowing rivers and potentially unstable riverbanks
during the high-streamflow period.

The River Forecast Centre continues to monitor the conditions and will provide updates as
conditions warrant.

A High Streamflow Advisory means that river levels are rising or expected to rise rapidly, but that no major flooding

is expected. Minor flooding in low-lying areas is possible.

A Flood Watch means that river levels are rising and will approach or may exceed bankfull. Flooding of areas

adjacent to affected rivers may occur.
