Home » FLNRO » River Forecast Centre Ministry of FLNRO Flood Warnings and Advisories Flood Watch – Central Interior, South Interior (UPGRADED) Flood Watch – Salmon River (MAINTAINED) High Streamflow Advisory –South-East BC (MAINTAINED) UPDATED: May 11, 2017 4:00 PM The River Forecast Centre is upgrading to a Flood Watch for: Central Interior and Thompson Region including the Coldwater River, Nicola River, Bonaparte River and small streams and rivers around Cache Creek, Merritt, Kamloops and surrounding areas Shuswap Region including small streams and rivers around Salmon Arm, Enderby, Sicamous and surrounding areas Okanagan including Mission Creek, small streams and rivers around Vernon, Lumby, Kelowna, Penticton and surrounding areas Similkameen including the Tulameen and Similkameen Rivers Boundary including the Kettle River, Granby River and small streams and rivers around Rock Creek, Greenwood, Grand Forks and surrounding areas The River Forecast Centre is maintaining a Flood Watch for: Salmon River The River Forecast Centre is maintaining a High Streamflow Advisory for: West Kootenay including small streams and rivers around Castlegar, Kaslo, Nelson, Salmo and surrounding areas East Kootenay including small streams and rivers around Creston, Cranbrook and surrounding areas Columbia including small streams and rivers around Revelstoke, Golden and surrounding areas Thundershowers are expected to affect the region this afternoon and overnight. Some rain has been picked up on the radar and at weather stations, particularly through the western side of the region, including the Similkameen, Nicola, Okanagan, Kamloops area and Shuswap. Conditions are expected to intensify through the afternoon and evening, with the potential for 20-30 mm of rain forecast through tomorrow. At the moment, the areas with a greater likelihood flood conditions include streams and rivers in the Thompson-Nicola, Similkameen, Okanagan, Shuswap, and Boundary regions. High elevation snow melt has been accelerating, and is contributing more runoff to rivers fed by high elevation terrain than last week. Saturated soils are present throughout the region due to persistent wet weather over the past two months, and this is expected to continue to exacerbate flow conditions. As a result, some regions may not experience the same degree of high flows as last weekend, while other areas may experience a greater response. Flows in rivers are expected to rise overnight Thursday and through Friday, with peak levels expected on Friday, or into Saturday on larger river systems. The River Forecast Centre will continue to monitor conditions and will update the advisory as conditions warrant. A High Streamflow Advisory means that river levels are rising or expected to rise rapidly, but that no major flooding is expected. Minor flooding in low-lying areas is possible. A Flood Watch means that river levels are rising and will approach or may exceed bankfull. Flooding of areas adjacent to affected rivers may occur. A Flood Warning means that river levels have exceeded bankfull or will exceed bankfull imminently, and that flooding of areas adjacent to the rivers affected will result.
