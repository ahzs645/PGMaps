Sampled cable length sums straight segments in the profile plane; it is not a purchase length or a catenary, tension, load, anchor, equipment or operating-safety calculation. Distances are horizontal along the resolved physical start-to-end direction. The support elevation difference is signed end minus start. Negative clearance means the assumed cable passes below sampled ground. A complete geometric profile does not establish a safe cable operation.
