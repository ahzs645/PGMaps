Quantity basis and limitations

Cut/fill areas are integrated from sampled cross-sections; interval volumes use average end areas. No-data or unclosed daylight sections leave an interval incomplete. Mass balance applies entered material reuse and compaction factors, or the default reusable-cut factor for unclassified ground. Costs use the entered rates, structures, haul scenario, mobilization and contingency where supplied; zero rates remain unpriced. Geotechnical treatments are not modeled. Ground is held separate from designed road elevation.

This is a preliminary planning record. It does not establish construction suitability, slope stability, drainage capacity, fish passage, permits or cable operating safety. D-lines screen assumed geometry without cable tension, load or anchor analysis. Crossing intersections require review. Entered culverts provide geometry and cover checks, not a hydraulic or fish-passage design.
